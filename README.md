**GL Player** is a free IPTV player for **Android phones, tablets and Android TV / Google TV / Fire TV boxes**. Add your own M3U, Xtream Codes or STB portal playlist and enjoy live TV with a fast TV guide, catch-up, favourites, parental control and beautiful themes – all designed to be used comfortably with a TV remote.

---

## 🚀 Overview

GL Player brings live TV, movies & series, the TV guide and all your settings into one clean app. It opens instantly on your last channel, shows what's on right away, and works the same on a phone in your hand or a box under your TV.

---

## 🔥 Key Features

### ✅ Playlists & Sources
- **M3U / M3U8** playlists – from a link or a file on your device
- **JSON** playlists
- **Xtream Codes** – server, username and password (categories, logos, catch-up and account info)
- **STB / MAG portals** – portal URL + MAC address (advanced: serial number, device IDs, model)
- **Open with GL Player** – open M3U files and stream links straight from your file manager or browser
- Use GL Player as the **external player** for other apps
- **Add from your phone** on TV – scan a QR code and send a link, an Xtream login, a file or an EPG from your phone
- Several playlists at once, each with its own guide, refresh options and tags (Local / Remote / Xtream / Stalker)
- Auto refresh, refresh at startup, or refresh a single playlist
- **Reset changes** per playlist – bring back removed channels, categories and original names

### ✅ Playback
- Powered by Media3 / ExoPlayer: **HLS, DASH (MPD), MPEG-TS, MP4, MKV** and more
- **DRM**: ClearKey (incl. multi-key) and Widevine via playlist `#KODIPROP` license tags
- Custom **User-Agent**, Referrer and cookies per playlist or channel
- **Hardware / software decoder**, tunneling, **Auto Frame Rate** (24/25/30/50/60 Hz)
- **Live delay / buffer** – starts close to live, with Small / Medium / Big buffer for weak connections
- Audio track and **subtitle** selection, aspect ratio (Fit / Fill / Zoom / 16:9 / 4:3)
- **Multiview** on TV – up to 4 channels at once
- **Picture-in-Picture** and full **gesture controls** on phone (volume, brightness, seek, zoom)
- Quality badges on the info bar: **FHD / HD / SD, frame rate (fps), Stereo / 5.1**

### ✅ Catch-up / Timeshift
- Replay past programmes straight from the TV guide (marked ⟲)
- Works with **Xtream Codes** archives (automatic), **Flussonic** servers and **M3U catch-up tags** (`catchup`, `catchup-days`, `catchup-source` with `{utc}` / `{utcend}`)

### ✅ TV Guide (EPG)
- **XMLTV** guides (`.xml` / `.xml.gz`) – from the playlist itself, Xtream, or any link or file you add
- Several guide sources per playlist, with update interval, **time offset** and days to keep
- **Advanced full-screen guide** on TV: channels, programmes, a days column and full details
- Opens on the **programme airing now**; press ► to read and scroll the full description
- Programme progress bars in the channel list
- **Instant guide at startup** – the info bar shows what's on from the first second

### ✅ Channels & Categories
- **Favourites** and favourite categories
- **Category manager** – hide, reorder, sort and create your own categories
- **Rename**, **remove** or **lock** channels (removed channels stay removed after refreshes)
- **ALL** category with every channel of a playlist
- Separate **VOD section** for movies & series
- **Search** channels across all playlists – and search **settings** too
- **History** of recently watched channels
- Channel numbers, ▲▼ zapping, number keys, BACK to the previous channel

### ✅ Made for Android TV
- Full remote control navigation, **hold-button shortcuts** you can customise
- **Info bar styles**: Default, Modern and a four-block bar with clock and logo
- **Themes**: Dark, Glass, Sky, Slate, Olive, Graphite, Light, Dev Favourite and a fully **Custom** theme
- Colour settings for text, channel names, numbers, guide, progress bar and more
- Logo shape: rectangle or **circle**
- **Start on boot**, start channel options and a fast cold start

### ✅ Made for Phones & Tablets
- Clean home screen with list, grid, compact or alternating playlist views
- Themes (Dark, Light, System, Glass, Sky, Slate, Olive, Graphite, two-tone), colour schemes and opacity settings
- **Chromecast** support *(Google Play version)*
- Play **local video files**
- Tools: playlist editor, checker and more
- Settings search

### ✅ Parental Control
- PIN protection for **locked channels and whole categories**

### ✅ Backup & Restore
- Full backup or **playlists & EPG only**
- Save to a file, or **backup / restore with your phone** by scanning a QR code on the TV

### ✅ 17 Languages
English, Albanian, Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (PT & BR), Russian, Spanish, Turkish, Ukrainian and Vietnamese.

### ✅ 100% Free
- ✅ No subscriptions or in-app purchases

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- 🚫 **GL Player does not include any channels, playlists or content.** You must add your own playlist from a provider you are entitled to use.
- 💡 GL Player is a media player only and is not affiliated with any IPTV provider or broadcaster.
- 🔒 Your playlist logins are stored only on your device and are sent only to your own provider.
